Salon policies

These policies keep our schedule running smoothly and fair for every guest. Thank you for your understanding.

  • Appointments & cancellations
  • Deposits
  • Pricing & payments
  • Privacy

Appointments & cancellations

  • All appointments are secured with a credit card on file.
  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged a fee of up to 50% of the booked service.
  • If you're running late, please call us. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled or shortened.

Deposits

A deposit is required to reserve color appointments longer than 2 hours, bridal services and extensions. The deposit goes toward your final bill. Deposit amounts and refund terms are shown during booking and your consultation.

Pricing & payments

  • Prices vary by stylist level, hair length and density. We give an estimate when you book and confirm the final price before your service begins.
  • We accept major credit cards, cash, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  • Gratuity is at your discretion and can be added by card.

Privacy

Your information is kept confidential. We never share your contact details, and we never post photos of you without your permission. We use your contact details only to manage your appointments and, if you opt in, to send offers you can unsubscribe from at any time.

A full privacy policy will be published here.

Questions about our policies? Contact us and we'll be glad to help.
