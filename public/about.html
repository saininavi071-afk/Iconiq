About us

Luxury hair care, redefined for Dallas

Iconiq began with one goal: to make every salon visit feel like an occasion, with expert artistry and genuine hospitality.

Our story

Artistry and community, in the heart of Uptown

We founded Iconiq Hair and Beauty to raise the standard of hair care in the city. Our salon combines skilled artistry with a warm, welcoming community, so every guest leaves looking polished and feeling at home.

Our mission is simple: to empower our clients and build confidence one hairstyle at a time. Every visit pairs personal attention with spa-like relaxation. Style, comfort and value should come together, and they do here.

What we believe

The Iconiq standard

Expertise first

Every stylist is hand-picked for their craft, whether color, extensions, bridal or barbering, and we invest in ongoing education so we stay ahead of the trends.

Healthy hair, always

We use premium professional products chosen with your hair's health in mind. Beautiful results should never come at the cost of damage.

Made for you

Trends are a starting point. We listen, consult and tailor each look to your features, lifestyle and routine, whether it's a classic refresh or a bold change.

Our team

Decades of combined experience

Our stylists bring decades of combined experience in precision cutting, dimensional color, extensions, bridal styling and men's grooming. We'll match you with the right specialist for your goals.

Clients tell us Iconiq feels like home. From the interior design to the service, every detail reflects our commitment to your beauty and well-being.
